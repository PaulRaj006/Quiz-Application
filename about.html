🎯 About PlacementPro

A lightweight, browser-based quiz platform built to help students revise placement-ready fundamentals — one focused, timed quiz at a time.

Project Objective

PlacementPro was built to give students a quick, distraction-free way to test themselves on core placement topics — General Knowledge, Data Structures, Python, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — under real quiz conditions with a live countdown timer.

Why PlacementPro

Most quiz apps rely on a backend or an account system. PlacementPro proves that a fast, polished, and genuinely useful prep tool can be built with nothing but HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — running entirely in the browser.

❓ FAQ

Do I need to sign up to use PlacementPro?
No account is needed. Just enter your name on the Home page and start a quiz — your results are saved locally in your browser.
Where is my data stored?
All quiz history and scores are stored in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to a server.
Will my data sync across devices?
Not currently — since there's no backend, data stays on the device and browser you used to take the quiz.
Can I retake a quiz?
Yes, you can retake any category and difficulty as many times as you like — every attempt is logged in your History.
